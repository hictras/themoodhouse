Our Story

Nestled within the historic stone archways of the picturesque Batroun Old Souk, The Mood House is a warmly curated boutique owned and managed by Gisane Yamak. We bring together a hand-picked collective of authentic Lebanese designers and artisanal makers, offering a sanctuary where local craftsmanship meets timeless Mediterranean elegance.
